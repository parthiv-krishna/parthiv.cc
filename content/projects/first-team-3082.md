I was an (over)involved member of my high school's FIRST Robotics Competition Team #3082 "Chicken Bot Pie". I held leadership positions in all four years, led a team of ~50 members, and helped establish long-term program sustainability. I was a finalist for the FIRST Dean's List Award twice.

### 2016 (Freshman) *Programming Lead; Operator*

- Led development of the team's first autonomous mode in many years.
- Controlled robot auxiliary subsystems during teleoperation. Semifinalists.

{{ img(path="static/img/frc-3082/robot-2016.jpg", alt="Team 3082's robot for the 2016 competition.") }}

### 2017 (Sophomore) *Captain; Programming Lead; Driver*

- Elected Captain - developed comprehensive recruitment and fundraising program that led to tripling of membership and budget.
- Incorporated computer vision in autonomous routines, leading to a 100% success rate of autonomous placement.
- Controlled robot drivetrain during teleoperation. Ranked 13th.

### 2018 (Junior) *Captain; Programming Lead; Driver*

- Continued to develop team membership, sponsorship, and community presence. Encouraged creation of robust outreach program.
- Implemented spline encoder feedback to place game pieces during autonomous mode. Integrated PID control loops to control linear elevator/lift mechanism to automatically reach target heights for scoring.
- Continued to control robot drivetrain, drove during the team's first ever finals appearance and World Championships appearance.

### 2019 (Senior) *Engineering Captain; Drive Coach*

- Relinquished role as Programming Lead to focus on overseeing a rapidly growing team (approaching 50 registered members).
- Oversaw system integration for the design, build, programming, and testing of the team's most ambitious robot ever.
- Designed the majority of our 2019 robot which included numerous unique features, such as a "swerve" drivetrain and a superstructure with three degrees of freedom.
- Wrote technical documentation to present to judges. Received the Innovation in Control Award at two events -- the first judged awards in our team's 10-year history ([PDF file](/pdf/frc3082-technical-documentation-2019.pdf)).
- Developed strategy for each match, communicated with other drive teams to ensure cohesive and collaborative robot operation during matches, represented the team on the field. Led team to first ever event win at the 2019 North Star Regional.
