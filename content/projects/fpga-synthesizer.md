For Stanford's class EE 108: Digital System Design, I worked on a final project with three teammates that involved creating a synthesizer on an FPGA. We programmed all aspects, including audio output, video output, and button input, using Verilog. Features that we implemented included:

- 4 programmable songs
- 4 simultaneous notes
- 4 instrument choices (harmonics + ADSR dynamics)
- Stereo effects: panning, phaser
- Visualize current waveform
- Visualize previous two, current, and next two notes on each track

Through the project, we gained a deeper understanding for aspects of digital design like combinational logic, sequential logic, timing, and pipelining.
