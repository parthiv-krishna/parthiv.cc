I'm a Stanford alum (BS and MS) with a passion for hardware and software. I have experience working on projects through coursework, research, and small and large companies, and have a particular interest in computer architecture for high-performance embedded computing.

I graduated from Stanford Tau Beta Pi and Phi Beta Kappa with my B.S. in Electrical Engineering in June 2023, as a Frederick Emmons Terman Scholar and a Henry Ford II Scholar. I focused on computer systems and signal processing on the Hardware and Software Systems track. I also completed a minor in Music, focusing on audio production and music theory. I found lots of interesting overlap between EE and Music, especially in the domain of signals.

I received my Master of Science in Computer Science at Stanford in June 2024. I was on the systems track and studied computer architecture, compilers, and embedded systems. I focused my coursework and research toward areas like hardware/software co-design, efficient embedded architectures, and domain-specific hardware.
