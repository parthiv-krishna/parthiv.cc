# Site authorship

> No AI text. AI assistance for infra/design is welcome, and for review of text, but all text must be human-authored.

This applies to all text published by the site, including titles, headings, labels, captions, alt text, metadata, and text embedded in templates or assets. It also applies when the same content is rendered in the TUI.

- AI may work on infrastructure, tooling, layouts, and styling. Preserve existing copy when changing how it is rendered.
- AI may review copy and flag issues, but must not draft, rewrite, paraphrase, translate, or silently correct publishable text. Give feedback as comments in the conversation, not as replacement copy.
- AI may migrate or format human-authored text without changing its wording when the maintainer identifies the source. If authorship is unclear or a feature needs new text, ask the maintainer for the exact wording before adding it.
- The maintainer must verify the provenance of any changed publishable text before merging or deploying it. Human approval of an AI draft does not make it human-authored. Do not use AI detectors as proof of authorship.
